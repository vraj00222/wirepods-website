Why not “any app”?

iOS sandboxes audio — no app can redirect Reels/YouTube app sound without jailbreak (none for A18/A19 on iOS 26). After pairing, play inside WirePods and it’s automatic. For Instagram directly, the fallback is still one tap: Control Center → Screen Mirroring → Mac. We don’t hide that.

Local only

Bonjour _wirepods._tcp + _wirepods-audio._tcp over your LAN. No cloud, no account, no third-party SDK. Heartbeat every 5s, 800 ms debounce.

How it works

1

Same Wi-Fi pairing

Open WirePodsMac (menu bar 🎧) and WirePodsiOS. Both advertise+browse Bonjour. One tap on the AirPlay picker pairs them — trust is persisted.

2

iPhone play → Mac buds

Hit play in the iPhone app. It broadcasts claim:iphone; Mac receives and pauses browser YouTube via AppleScript. AirPlay carries audio to the wired buds.

3

Mac play → auto back

When YouTube starts on the Mac, SystemAudioMonitor detects CoreAudio activity and broadcasts claim:mac. iPhone ducks automatically.

Main flow you need: iPhone → Mac wired buds via AirPlay. No Screen Recording needed because you’re looking at the iPhone screen.

FAQ

Do I need to tap every time?

No. One tap on the AirPlay picker during first pairing. iOS remembers the route; next launches auto-connect. Same-WiFi discovery is automatic via Bonjour.

Does it cover Instagram Reels directly?

Only inside WirePods’ own player (AVAudioSession wall). For Reels in Instagram, stock iOS fallback is still Control Center → Screen Mirroring → Mac — we document it honestly. No jailbreak for iPhone 17/A18 on iOS 26 today.

Why same Wi-Fi?

Safety + no cloud. Discovery is LAN-only. Both devices must be on the same network (or iPhone via cable) — keeps pairing private and latency low.

M4 + iPhone 17 supported?

Yes — that’s the primary test target. WirePodsMac builds arm64,WirePodsiOS builds iphoneos/iphonesimulator, deployment targets 14.0/17.0.
